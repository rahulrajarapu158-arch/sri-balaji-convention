The Grand Kalyana Mandapam

Features an open, pillarless design ensuring every guest has an unobstructed view of the wedding rituals and sacred fire. Equipped with multi-tier central AC, acoustic wall panelling, and ceremonial lighting tracks.

  • Elevated Mandap Stage: 42ft wide platform with dedicated priest and family seating zones.
  • Acoustic Optimization: Tuned reverberation for crystal-clear priest chants (Veda Mantras) and nadaswaram.
  • Cinematic Illumination: Warm golden cove lighting and high-CRI fixtures tailored for wedding videography.

Bhojana Shala (Dining Hall)

Spacious, highly hygienic dining facility configured for both traditional South Indian leaf service (Banthi Bhojanam) and contemporary buffet catering.

  • Banthi & Buffet Flow: Two parallel wings allowing simultaneous seated dining and buffet counters.
  • Heavy Exhaust & Fresh Air: Industrial exhaust ducts ensuring the dining hall remains cool and fresh.
  • Commercial Prep Kitchen: High-pressure gas bank, RO water plant, and dedicated wash area for catering teams.
